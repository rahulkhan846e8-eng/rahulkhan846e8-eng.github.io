Privacy Policy

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Shinobi Hub (shinobihub.run.place) respects your personal privacy. We do not require registration, passwords, or personal payment credentials to search, browse, or stream anime.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, physical addresses, passwords, phone numbers, or credit card details. We will never sell, lease, or distribute your private information to third-party data brokers.

2. Standard Log Files & Web Analytics

Like virtually all modern web services, standard server log files may be generated by our web hosting infrastructure. These files record non-personally identifiable technical information such as:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses and Internet Service Providers (ISP)
  • Browser type, engine version, and operating system
  • Date and time stamps of requests
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Aggregate, non-identifiable page visit counts

This technical data is used exclusively to analyze site stability, defend against DDoS attacks, and optimize loading performance.

3. Browser Local Storage (localStorage)

Shinobi Hub does not use tracking cookies to track users across the web. We use standard browser localStorage strictly on your local device to power essential client-side features:

  • Your Personal Watchlist: Keeping track of anime you have bookmarked.
  • Episode Playback Position & History: Remembering your playback progress so you can resume where you left off ("Continue Watching").
  • Player Preferences: Saving your volume level, muted state, and preferred audio language (Hindi/English).

This data resides exclusively on your local device and is never uploaded or synchronized to remote servers. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings or using the "Clear History" button on the site.

4. Third-Party Advertising & External Links

Shinobi Hub may display third-party advertisements or link to external file hosting platforms to help support hosting expenses. These third-party advertisers or external services may use cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons governed by their own independent privacy policies. Shinobi Hub has no access to or control over cookies used by third-party services.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

Under the CCPA, California consumers are entitled to know what personal data is collected and have the right to request its deletion. Shinobi Hub does not sell personal information. To exercise any privacy rights, contact us at shinobihub7@gmail.com.

6. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance

Visitors from the European Union (EU) have rights to access, rectification, erasure, and data portability. Because Shinobi Hub does not store identifiable personal accounts, you may manage and erase all local data simply by clearing your browser cache and local storage.

7. Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions or require additional information regarding our Privacy Policy, please email us directly at shinobihub7@gmail.com.
